## Compact or Expanded

The Inbox shows one item at a time on the triage card. The card is **Compact** by default, so
it fits a phone screen without scrolling: the kind of action, project, due date and context
are on it, and any notes the item already has show under its title. Notes and tags are
reached through **Edit**.

**Expanded** puts notes, tags and the project's purpose and outcome on the card itself. Switch
with the **Triage card** choice at the bottom of the Inbox. The choice is saved on this
device only, so a phone can stay Compact while a laptop is Expanded.

## Delete

**Delete** takes the item out of the inbox and puts it in Done → Dropped. An **Undo?** button
shows under the card for seven seconds and puts it straight back. After that, reopen it from
the Done tab's Dropped list. A todo is never erased.
