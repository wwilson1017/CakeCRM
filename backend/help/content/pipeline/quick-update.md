## Where it is

Choose **Quick update** in the CRM navigation. The screen works on a phone as well as a desktop.

## Finding the deal

Type part of a deal title, company or contact name, then pick the deal. Open deals come first.
Closed deals are greyed but still pickable, so you can correct a wrong close by choosing an open stage.
Any member can update any live deal; ownership is an assignment, not a permission.

## The actions

Turn on **Note**, **Value**, **Stage** or **Follow-up**, fill in what changed, then press **Save**.
You can combine the tiles in one save. A note records the touch with the customer. The value keeps
the deal's currency. Stage offers the open stages only. A follow-up becomes a todo owned by you,
linked to the deal and its contact, in your Next actions.

## Won and Lost

**Mark Won** asks which day the deal closed: today or earlier, never a future day.
**Mark Lost** asks for an optional reason. A supplied reason is also added to the deal's notes.
That reason note is a record of the close, not a customer touch.
Both buttons include any note, value and follow-up you have filled in; the close sets the stage.

## Or just tell Baker

When an AI provider is connected, write one sentence under **Or just tell Baker** and press **Ask**.
For example: “sent samples, move to Proposal, value 2500”. Baker shows a preview of exactly what
will change on the selected deal. Nothing saves until **Confirm**, which uses the same save path
as the buttons. **Cancel** discards the preview.

Baker copies your words for the note. If your sentence does not say what happened, the preview
asks you for one line before Confirm is available. Check every preview line before confirming.
A Won preview names the exact closing day (today when you asked); that date stays fixed through
confirmation. Use Mark Won to choose another day. An already-won or already-lost deal is not closed
again by the preview, and an existing lost reason is not rewritten.

A confirmed preview is your edit, so no “Baker wrote this” badge appears. With no AI provider,
the sentence box is hidden and all the other actions still work. Baker in the chat can make the
same changes through its own tools.

## If something fails

A refused save saves nothing: the fields, notes and follow-up are one transaction. Your typed
fields stay on screen so you can fix the refusal. A connection or server failure may leave you
unsure whether the save completed; open the deal and check before trying again. Nothing is retried
automatically.
