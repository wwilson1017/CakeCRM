The **Today** panel sits at the top of the [Dashboard](01-dashboard.md). It answers one question: what should I do now? It is ranked by plain rules, not AI, and works the same with or without an AI provider.

## What goes on it, in order

1. **Starred** todos you have marked for today.
2. **Hot deals that have gone quiet**: deals you have marked hot that have had no touch for 14 days or more. At most two take a slot, longest-quiet first, so a neglected pipeline never buries the promises you made yourself.
3. **Overdue** todos.
4. Todos **due today**.

Each todo row wears a label saying why it is there: STARRED, OVERDUE or DUE TODAY. A deal row shows its hot icon and how long it has been idle and what it is worth, for example "idle 12d · $30K".

## Five at a time

The panel shows up to five rows. If more are waiting, **+N more today** opens the rest and **Show less** folds them away again. The extra rows also include:

- Hot deals that are still being worked, and quiet hot deals beyond the first two.
- Todos marked BROUGHT BACK, whose bring-back date has arrived.

When nothing is ranked, the panel reads "Nothing needs you today."

## Acting on a row

- Tick the box on a todo row to complete it without leaving the dashboard.
- Click a todo's title to go to [Todos](../05-todos/01-todo-modes.md).
- Click a deal to open it on the [Pipeline](../03-pipeline/03-deal-page.md).

Marking a deal hot is up to you; see [Deal health and scores](../03-pipeline/07-deal-health-and-scores.md).

## Mine and Everyone

On an install with more than one person, **MINE** and **EVERYONE** buttons sit beside the heading. Mine shows your own work plus anything with no owner, so unassigned work is always caught by someone; those rows are labelled Unassigned. Everyone shows the whole team. Your choice is remembered in this browser tab.

## When it updates

The panel uses the install's own day, not your computer's, and reloads itself at midnight.
