CakeCRM has a light theme and a dark theme. Every page works the same in both; only the look changes.

{{shot:dark-mode}}

## Switching

Press the sun or moon button in the header, beside the notifications bell. It is in the header on a phone as well.

- In light mode the button shows a moon and switches you to dark.
- In dark mode it shows a sun and switches you to light.

## How CakeCRM picks a theme

Until you press the button, CakeCRM follows your device's own setting. If your computer switches to dark mode in the evening, CakeCRM switches with it.

Once you press the button, your choice wins and stays put. It is saved in this browser, so:

- Other tabs of CakeCRM open in the same browser switch along with it.
- Another browser or another device keeps its own setting.
- If your browser blocks site storage, the choice lasts until you close the tab.

The theme is a personal display choice. It does not affect anyone else on your team.
