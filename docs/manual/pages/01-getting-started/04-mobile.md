CakeCRM works in your phone's browser. There is nothing to install; open the same address you use on a computer and sign in.

{{shot:mobile-dashboard}}

## Getting around

On a narrow screen the header changes:

- A row of tabs under the header holds the six destinations: Dashboard, Pipeline, Contacts, Companies, Todos and Reports. Swipe it sideways if they do not all fit.
- The light/dark switch and the notifications bell stay in the header, so alerts are always one tap away.
- The menu button (three lines, top left) opens the **Menu** drawer.

{{shot:mobile-menu}}

The drawer lists the same six destinations plus **Settings**, **AI Setup** (admins only) and **Sign out**. Tap the **×** to close it.

## The pipeline on a phone

The board shows roughly one stage column at a time, and you swipe between them. A row of stage pills above the board jumps straight to a column and shows which one you are on.

Some desktop actions work differently on a phone:

- **No dragging.** To move a deal to another stage, open it and use **Edit**, or **Mark Won** or **Mark Lost**.
- **No checkboxes**, so there is no bulk move. Use a computer for [moving deals in bulk](../03-pipeline/05-bulk-moves.md).
- The **Add Deal** button reads **Add**.
- A stage's checklist opens as a sheet along the bottom of the screen rather than a panel in the corner.

## Baker on a phone

The Baker button sits in the bottom-right corner on a phone too. Pages leave room at the bottom so you can scroll past it.
