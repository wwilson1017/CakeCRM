Every contact and company page, and every deal, has a **Chatter** section. It is a shared thread of notes that anyone on the team can add to.

## Adding a note

Type in **Add a note… (type @ to mention a teammate)** and post it. A note counts as being in touch, so adding one updates a contact's **Last contact** date.

## Mentioning a teammate

Type `@` and pick a name from the list. That person gets a notification. Its title opens the record. You are never notified when you mention yourself. If you edit a note later, only people newly added to it are notified.

See [Notifications](../01-getting-started/06-notifications.md) for how notifications reach you.

## Attaching files

Press **Attach** in the note composer to add files to a note:

- Up to **10 MB** per file.
- Up to **10 files** per note.

Images that the app can preview show as a small tile. Click it to see a larger view. Other files show as a chip that downloads when you click it. You can remove an attachment from its note.

Attachments work with no AI provider connected.

## Editing and archiving notes

Each note has **Edit** and **Archive**. An edited note is marked "· edited". An archived note is hidden from the thread. **Show archived** brings archived notes back into view, marked "· archived", each with **Restore**.

## Notes field versus Chatter

A contact or company also has a single **Notes** field on its form. It shows in a card near the top of the page. Use it for a standing summary, such as "Weekly bread and pastry buyer." Use Chatter for the running story.
