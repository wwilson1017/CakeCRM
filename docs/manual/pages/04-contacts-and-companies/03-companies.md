Open **Companies** in the top navigation. The table shows each company's name, industry, domain, phone and status.

{{shot:companies-list}}

## Searching, filtering and sorting

**Search companies…** looks at the name, domain, industry, phone, address and notes. **Filters** offers Status, Industry and, on a team of more than one, Owner (including **Unassigned**). **Sort** offers Name (the default), Industry, Domain, Status and Recently updated. **Views** saves a search and filters for the whole team, just as on Contacts.

## A company's page

Click a company to open it.

{{shot:company-detail}}

The header shows the name and status, then the industry, domain, phone and address. The **Owner** row always shows, even when it is *Unassigned*.

Below come:

- **Notes** — the company's notes field, if it has one.
- **Contacts** — everyone linked to this company, with job title and email. Click one to open them.
- **Deals** — every deal linked to the company, with **Open pipeline** showing the total value of its open deals.
- **Activity History** — notes and logged activity from the company's contacts and deals in one feed, each marked with where it came from.
- **Custom fields** — if an admin has defined any for companies.
- **Chatter** — a notes thread for the company. See [Notes, mentions and attachments](05-notes-mentions-attachments.md).

**Edit** opens the company form. **Delete** asks you to confirm, then permanently deletes the company. Contacts and deals linked to it are kept and unlinked. To hide a company without losing anything, archive it instead. See [Archiving](08-archiving.md).

For a printable summary of one company, see [The company report](../06-reports/01-company-report.md).
