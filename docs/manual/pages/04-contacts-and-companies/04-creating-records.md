Press **+ Add Contact** on the Contacts page or **+ Add Company** on the Companies page.

{{shot:add-contact}}

## What is required

Very little:

- **Contact** — only a **Name**. Email, phone, company, job title, source, status, owner, tags and notes are all optional.
- **Company** — only a **Name**. Domain, industry, phone, address, source, status, owner and notes are optional.

Tags on a contact are typed as a comma-separated list. **Owner** starts on you. See [Ownership](09-ownership.md) for what it means.

A new contact can be Active or Inactive, and a new company is Active. Neither can be created already archived. Create it first, then archive it with a reason.

## Linking a company from a contact

The **Company** field on the contact form is a search-as-you-type picker. Type a name and pick the company from the list. If nothing matches, the list offers a **Create "…"** row. Choosing it creates the company straight away and links it.

Two things to know:

- **Typing a name and pressing Save without choosing a row discards it.** The pick, or the Create row, is what counts.
- Creating from the picker saves a real company at once. If you then cancel the form, that company stays. Typing the same name later reuses it rather than making a second one.

Company names are matched without regard to capitals or extra spaces, so "The Green Table" and "the green table " are the same company.

## The New Company form

The **+ Add Company** form always creates a new company. If that name already exists, it tells you so plainly.

## An old unlinked company name

A contact may carry a company name as plain text that was never linked to a company record. The form shows that text with a "not linked" hint and a Remove action. If you leave the field alone while editing something else, the text is kept.

## Adding deals and todos from a record

The **+ Add** links on a contact's Deals and Todos lists open a new deal or todo already linked to that person. For deals, see [Adding a deal](../03-pipeline/08-adding-a-deal.md).
