Click a name on the Contacts list to open that person's page. **← Contacts** takes you back to the list.

{{shot:contact-detail}}

## The header

The top shows the name and score, then the job title and the company. The company is a link to its own page. Below that come the email, phone, **Owner** and tags. The owner row always shows, even when it reads *Unassigned*.

**Edit** opens the contact form. **Delete** asks you to confirm, then permanently deletes the contact and their activity history. It cannot be undone. To keep the history, archive the contact instead. See [Archiving](08-archiving.md).

## Notes

If the contact has a **Notes** field filled in, it shows in a card under the header. You change it through **Edit**.

## Deals and Todos

Two side-by-side lists show the deals and todos linked to this person. Each has **+ Add**, which opens a new deal or todo already linked to the contact. A deal card shows its title, stage and value. A todo shows its due date and priority, with done todos struck through.

## Log Activity

Pick **Call**, **Email**, **Meeting** or **Note**, type what happened in **Add a note...**, and press **Log**. It appears in **Activity History** below, newest first. Logging activity updates the contact's **Last contact** date.

## Custom fields and Chatter

If an admin has defined custom fields for contacts, a Custom fields section shows them here. See [Custom fields](10-custom-fields.md).

**Chatter** is the running notes thread for the contact. You can mention teammates and attach files there. See [Notes, mentions and attachments](05-notes-mentions-attachments.md).
