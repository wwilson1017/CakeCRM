Archiving keeps a record and everything on it, while marking it as no longer current. For contacts and companies, **Archived** is a status, alongside Active (and Inactive, for contacts).

Deals are archived differently. See [Archived deals](../03-pipeline/06-archived-deals.md).

## How to archive

1. Open the contact or company and press **Edit**.
2. Set **Status** to **Archived**.
3. Fill in **Why archive?**. Saving is refused until there is a reason.
4. Save.

With an AI provider connected, you can also ask Baker to archive a record. It asks you why if you have not said. In Baker's **Ask** mode, an archive always waits for your approval. See [Approvals](../07-baker/03-approvals.md).

A record cannot be created already archived. Create it first, then archive it.

## What is kept

The record keeps every note, activity and deal. You can still find it: on the Contacts or Companies list, filter **Status** to Archived.

## What is recorded

Archiving adds an "Archived — *reason*" note to the record, credited to you. Setting the status back to Active (or Inactive) adds a "Restored from archive." note.

Neither note counts as being in touch, so a contact's **Last contact** date does not change.

## Archive or delete?

**Delete** removes a record for good and cannot be undone. Deleting a contact also deletes their activity history. Archive unless you are sure you will never need the record again.
