Open **Contacts** in the top navigation. The table shows each person's name and job title, company, email, phone, when you were last in touch, their status and their score.

{{shot:contacts-list}}

## Searching

Type in **Search contacts…** to narrow the table as you type. Search looks at the name, email, phone, company, job title, tags and notes.

## Filters

**Filters** opens these choices. Use as many as you like at once:

| Filter | What it does |
|---|---|
| Status | Active, Inactive or Archived |
| Tags | Any tag used on a contact |
| Owner | A teammate, or **Unassigned** (only shown when there is more than one person on the team) |
| Score | Hot (70+), Warm (40-69), Cool (under 40) or Not scored |
| Last contact | Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Not in 30 days, or Never |

The count beside the filters, such as "11 of 11 contacts", always matches the rows on screen.

## Sorting

Pick a field from **Sort**: Recently updated (the default), Name, Company, Email, Score, Last contact or Status. The arrow button beside it flips the order. You can also click a column heading.

## Views

**Views** saves the current search, filters and sort under a name. Your whole team can apply a saved view. See [List filters and views](../03-pipeline/02-list-filters-and-views.md) for how views work across the app.

## Last contact and score

**Last contact** is worked out from logged activity and Chatter notes on the contact. It moves when someone logs a call, email or meeting, or adds a note. The **score** is a number from 0 to 100, worked out without AI. See [Deal health and scores](../03-pipeline/07-deal-health-and-scores.md).

## "Showing saved data"

On a large CRM the full list can take a moment to load. Contacts opens on the rows you saw last time, with a line saying **Refreshing… showing saved data from** a time. The fresh list then replaces them. If loading fails, the line reads **Couldn't refresh** and offers **Retry**. These saved rows stay in your browser only. They are erased when you sign out and thrown away after seven days.

The round-arrow button beside **Import Contacts** reloads the list on demand.
