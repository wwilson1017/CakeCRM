## The Contexts tab

**Contexts** lets you browse todos grouped by context, each heading with a count. Todos without one sit under **No context**.

{{shot:todos-contexts}}

Use the **All contexts** list at the top right to show a single context.

Press **Rename** beside a heading to rename that context on every todo at once, or to merge it into another. See [To Do](05-to-do.md) for how renaming and merging work.

## Choosing contexts

A context says where or how you can do something. By habit it starts with an at-sign, such as @calls, @computer, @errands or @office. When you set a context, the app offers the ones already in use, plus **+ New context…**.

Keep the list short. Five to eight contexts usually cover everything.

## Searching all todos

The **Search…** box at the top right of the Todos page searches every todo, whatever its tab. Results appear on their own page under "Results for" what you typed, and the box keeps your search.

A todo hidden by a **Bring back on** date still turns up in search, labelled with the day it comes back.

## Searching within a tab

Most tabs also have their own box, such as **Search inbox…** or **Filter…**, which only narrows that tab. Someday, Done and Projects add **Filters** and **Views**, the same as the Contacts list.
