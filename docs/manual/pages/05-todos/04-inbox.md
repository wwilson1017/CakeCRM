Everything you capture starts in the **Inbox**. The tab shows a red count of what is waiting. Your goal is to get it to zero.

{{shot:todos-inbox}}

The item at the top of the queue opens as a card with three steps.

## 01 What kind of action?

Pick one:

- **Next action** — you will do it yourself. It goes to [To Do](05-to-do.md).
- **Waiting** — you are waiting on someone else.
- **Delegated** — you handed it to someone.
- **Someday** — not now.

Picking one saves nothing yet. The item stays on the card while you add detail.

If the whole thing takes under two minutes, just do it and press **Took 2 minutes — Done ✓**.

## 02 Add detail (optional)

- **Project** — pick one, or choose **+ New project…**. On the Expanded card, the card shows that project's purpose and outcome, so you can check it is the right home.
- **Due date** — only for a real deadline.
- **Bring back on** — hides the todo until that day, then shows it on Today.
- **Notes** and **Tags** — on the Expanded card only. Notes hold links, numbers, anything you will want when you do it; tags are comma-separated, like `errand, quick`, and the ones you already use show below the box. On the Compact card, any notes the item already has show under its title, and **Edit** reaches both.
- **Edit** opens the full editor, once any note, date or tags you just changed have saved. **Delete** takes the item out of the inbox: it moves to Done → Dropped, where you can reopen it, and **Undo?** brings it straight back. A todo is never erased.

The card is **Compact** by default, so it fits on a phone screen. The **Triage card** choice at the bottom of the Inbox switches this device to **Expanded**, which adds notes, tags and the project's purpose and outcome to the card.

Each field is labelled on the card, and each saves as you leave it. None of them takes the item out of the inbox.

## 03 Last step — set context

Choose a context from **Set context…**, or **+ New context…**. Choosing one files the item into the list you picked in step 01 and clears it out of the inbox. That is why it comes last.

## Undo

After filing or deleting, an **Undo?** button appears right under the card for seven seconds. It puts the item back exactly as it was.

## Finding things in the inbox

Use **Search inbox…** and **Filters** above the card. Each item carries a label if it came from Baker, the capture link or Telegram.
