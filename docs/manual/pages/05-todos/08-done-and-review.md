## Done

The **Done** tab lists finished todos.

{{shot:todos-done}}

- The **Done** and **Dropped** buttons switch between completed todos and ones you abandoned.
- **Search finished todos…**, **Filters** (by context) and **Views** work as on the other lists.
- Untick a todo to reopen it.

Dropping a todo, rather than deleting it, keeps it here where you can recover it.

## Review

The **Review** tab supports the weekly review: empty the inbox, give every active project a next action, follow up on stale waiting-fors and prune someday/maybe.

{{shot:todos-review}}

It shows:

- **Counts** of Inbox, Next, Waiting, Someday and Done todos.
- **Stale (14+ days untouched)** — next actions, waiting-for and delegated items nothing has touched in a while, oldest first.
- **Active projects without a next action.**
- **Review due** when no review has been marked done in the last seven days, or ever.

When you finish, press **Mark review done**. That records today and clears the **Review due** hint for a week.

## Reviewing with Baker

With an AI provider connected, ask Baker to start your weekly review. On the Review tab the assistant offers "Start my weekly review". Baker walks you through nine steps one at a time: a mind dump, clearing the inbox, looking back at the week, due items, projects, waiting items, stale next actions, someday/maybe, and a final look at your next actions. It makes the changes you agree to as you go.

Baker cannot press **Mark review done** for you. A review only counts once you have finished it.
