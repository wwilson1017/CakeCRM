## Waiting

The **Waiting** tab has two sections:

- **Waiting For** — things you are waiting on from someone else.
- **Delegated** — things you handed to someone and need to follow up.

{{shot:todos-waiting}}

Each item shows how long since it was last touched, so you can see what is ripe for a chase. Write who and what in the title, such as "Hannah Lee: signed order form for the fundraiser".

When the thing arrives or it is your move again, press **→ Next**. That makes it a next action on your [To Do](05-to-do.md) list.

Type in **Filter…** to narrow the list.

## Someday

The **Someday** tab holds ideas and maybes that are not commitments yet.

{{shot:todos-someday}}

- **→ Next** promotes an item to a next action when you are ready.
- **Filters** narrows by context.
- **Views** saves a search and filters for the whole team.

Look through Someday during your weekly review. Promote what is ready and drop what no longer matters.

## Filing items here

Choose **Waiting**, **Delegated** or **Someday** when you sort an item in the [Inbox](04-inbox.md), or change a todo's status in its editor.
