Everything you want to come back to lives in one place: your todos. The **Todos** page can show them in two ways.

| Mode | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Todo-GTD** (the default) | The full Getting Things Done workflow: an inbox to sort, next actions, waiting-for and delegated items, someday/maybe, projects, contexts, tags, stars, repeats and a weekly review. The Todos page has nine tabs. |
| **Simple list** | Just todos with due dates and priorities, in one table. No inbox or contexts. |

{{shot:todos-today}}

## Switching changes nothing about your data

Both modes read the **same** todos. Switching:

- moves, copies and deletes nothing,
- takes effect straight away,
- can be undone by switching back.

A todo made in one mode is the same todo in the other. GTD simply uses extra fields, like context and project, that the simple list leaves alone.

## Who can switch

**Admins only**, in **Settings → Assistant → Todo mode**. The mode is one setting for the whole team, so switching it changes the Todos page for everyone. See [Assistant settings](../08-settings/02-assistant.md).

## What else changes

With an AI provider connected, Baker's todo abilities follow the mode. In GTD mode it works with inboxes, contexts and projects. In Simple list mode it works with plain todos.

Repeating todos work in both modes: completing one creates the next.
