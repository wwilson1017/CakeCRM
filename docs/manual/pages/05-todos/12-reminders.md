A reminder is a note you write once that is delivered, word for word, to the people you choose at a date and optional time. It is not a todo: it never appears in todo lists and there is nothing to finish. Use a todo for work you need to do; use a reminder for "tell these people this, then". Reminders live on the **Reminders** page.

## Setting one

Click **Add Reminder**, write the note, pick a date and optionally a time, choose who to notify (nobody means you), and optionally a repeat: daily, weekly, monthly or yearly. A date-only reminder is sent at 9:00 in the morning. A date before today, or a time today that has already passed, is refused.

Each person gets an in-app notification, plus a browser push or a Telegram message if they turned those on. **A reminder is never sent by email**, and no AI runs when it goes out.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Upcoming** | Waiting to be sent. |
| **Paused** | Held. It is not sent until someone resumes it. |
| **Sent** | Delivered. It stays as a record. |
| **Voided** | Cancelled with a reason before it was sent. Voiding a repeating reminder ends it. |

Nothing is ever deleted. An upcoming or paused reminder can be edited or voided; a sent or voided one is history. **Remind again** copies any reminder's note and people to a new date.

## Pausing and resuming

Open a reminder and click **Pause** to hold it. A paused repeating reminder skips every date that comes round while it is paused. Click **Resume** to start it again:

- a **repeating** reminder picks up at its next date, without sending the ones it missed;
- a **one-time** reminder whose time passed while it was paused is sent at the next check, usually within a minute.

Any teammate can pause, resume, edit or void any reminder; who set one is not a lock.

## Asking Baker

Baker can set, list, change, pause, resume and void reminders. Every change except listing shows an Approve card first, because it decides who is notified and when. See [Approvals and modes](../07-baker/03-approvals.md).
