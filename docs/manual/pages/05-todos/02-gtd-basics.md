GTD, short for Getting Things Done, rests on one habit: get everything out of your head into a trusted list, then decide what each item means. CakeCRM's GTD mode is built around that.

## The workflow in four steps

1. **Capture everything.** Type it into the **Add to inbox** box at the top of the Todos page. Sort it out later.
2. **Clear the inbox.** For each item, decide what kind of action it is and where you can do it. See [The Inbox](04-inbox.md).
3. **Work from your lists.** [Today](03-today.md) shows what is due and starred. [To Do](05-to-do.md) shows next actions grouped by context.
4. **Review weekly.** The [Review](08-done-and-review.md) tab shows what needs attention.

## Quick add

The **Add to inbox** box understands a few shortcuts. As you type, each one it recognises appears as a chip under the box.

{{shot:todos-quick-add}}

| Type | Means |
|---|---|
| `#Wholesale` | File under the project "Wholesale" (created if it does not exist) |
| `@calls` | Context @calls |
| `!` | Star it for today |
| `today`, `tomorrow`, `next week`, `in 3 days`, `friday`, `aug 15`, `8/15`, `2026-08-15` | Due date |
| `daily`, `weekly`, `every weekday`, `every 3 days`, `every monday` | Repeat |

Click the ✕ on a chip if it guessed wrong. The words go back into the title. Everything you add this way lands in the inbox. "Today" and "tomorrow" follow the install's time zone, not your computer's clock.

## The fields on a todo

- **Status** — Inbox, next action, waiting for, delegated, someday/maybe, done or dropped. Dropped means abandoned but recoverable.
- **Context** — where or how you can do it, such as @calls, @computer or @errands.
- **Project** — an outcome that needs more than one action.
- **Due date** — for a real deadline only.
- **Bring back on** — hides the todo from your lists until that day, then shows it on Today. It is not a deadline.
- **Repeat** — daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly, yearly or every N days.
- **Star** — today's priorities. Keep it to a handful.
- **Tags** and **Notes** — anything else.
- **Links** — a todo can point at a contact and a deal.

Click a todo's title to open it and change any of these.

## Where a todo came from

A todo nobody typed into the app carries a small label: **Baker**, **Observer** (Baker noticed a promise in a conversation and filed it), **Capture link** or **Telegram**. Read a **Capture link** item before you act on it, because anyone with that link could have written it.

## Undo

Marking a todo done, or filing an inbox item, shows an **Undo?** button for seven seconds.
