**Today** is the first tab on the Todos page. It gathers four kinds of todo into sections:

| Section | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Overdue** | Todos whose due date has passed, in red |
| **Due today** | Todos due today |
| **Brought back** | Todos whose **Bring back on** date has arrived |
| **★ Starred** | Todos you starred as today's priorities |

When nothing qualifies, the page reads "Nothing on today's plate".

You can see the Today tab in the picture on [Todo modes](01-todo-modes.md).

## Working the list

- Tick the box to mark a todo done. An **Undo?** button appears for seven seconds.
- Click the star to star or unstar it.
- Click the title to open and edit it.

Chips under a todo show what it is linked to, such as a deal, a contact or a project.

## What "today" means

"Today" is the day in the time zone set for the whole install, not your own computer's clock. Someone working late in another time zone sees the same Overdue and Due today split as everyone else.

## Stars

A star means "do this today." Keep starred items to a handful. On a repeating todo you can also choose to star each new copy automatically when it comes due.

## Brought back is not overdue

A todo with a **Bring back on** date leaves every working list until that day, then appears under **Brought back**. That date is not a deadline, so a todo is never marked overdue because of it.
