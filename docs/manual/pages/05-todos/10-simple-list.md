When an admin sets the todo mode to **Simple list**, the Todos page becomes a single table. See [Todo modes](01-todo-modes.md).

{{shot:todos-simple-list}}

## The table

Each row has a done checkbox, the todo with what it is linked to (a contact and a deal), its **Priority** (High, Medium or Low) and its **Due** date. Overdue dates show in red.

Tick a box to mark a todo done. Click a todo to open it.

## Adding a todo

Press **+ Add Todo** and fill in:

- **What needs to be done?** (required)
- **Description**
- **Priority**
- **Due Date**
- **Contact**

You can also add a todo from a contact's page with **+ Add** next to Todos.

## Filters and sorting

**Filters** offers:

| Filter | Choices |
|---|---|
| Show | Open (the default), Done or All |
| Due | Overdue, Due today, Next 7 days, No due date |
| Priority | High, Medium, Low |
| Owner | A teammate or Unassigned, on a team of more than one |

**Sort** offers Open first, then due (the default), Due date, Priority, Title and Recently added. **Clear filters** resets them. **Views** saves a search and filters for the whole team.

## Repeating todos

A repeating todo still repeats in this mode. Ticking it creates the next one.
