**Pipeline** shows your deals as cards in columns, one column per stage. Under the page title a line reads how much is open and how many open deals there are.

{{shot:pipeline-board}}

## The columns

There are six stages: lead, qualified, proposal, negotiation, won and lost. The board opens with the four open stages showing. Won and Lost are hidden by default, so the board is about work that is still live.

When any stage is hidden, the line under the title says how many, with a **Show all** link beside it. Nothing is ever secretly missing. To hide or show a single column, use the stage checkboxes in the toolbar above the board. See [Stages and closing a deal](04-stages-and-closing.md) for how to make Won and Lost show by default.

## What a card shows

- The deal title and its value.
- The linked contact.
- The probability, if one is set.
- The forecasted close date, or "No forecast date".
- A lead score, an estimated touch count (only with an AI provider) and a temperature icon you can click to mark the deal hot, warm or cold.

On a Won card, the score, touch count and temperature are replaced by when the account was last contacted, because after the sale the question is whether it has gone quiet.

See [Deal health and scores](07-deal-health-and-scores.md) for what the numbers mean.

## Moving a deal

Drag a card into another column. The move saves straight away. Dragging still works while a filter is on.

Dragging into Won records today as the deal's Closed on date, and dragging into Lost records no reason. To capture the real close date or a lost reason, open the deal and use **Mark Won** or **Mark Lost** instead. See [Stages and closing a deal](04-stages-and-closing.md).

On a phone there is no dragging; open the deal instead. See [Using CakeCRM on a phone](../01-getting-started/04-mobile.md).

## Opening a deal

Click a card to open the deal in a panel over the board. See [The deal page](03-deal-page.md).

## Searching and filtering

The search box above the board matches deal titles, contact names and company names. **Filters** narrows the board by stage, owner, value, dates, activity and more. **Board** and **List** switch between this view and a table. See [List, filters and saved views](02-list-filters-and-views.md).

## Adding a deal

Press **Add Deal** at the top right. See [Adding a deal](08-adding-a-deal.md).
