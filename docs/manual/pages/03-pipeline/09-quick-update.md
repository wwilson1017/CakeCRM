## Find the deal

Open **Quick update** from the CRM navigation, on your phone or desktop. Type part of a title,
company or contact name and pick the deal. Open deals appear first. Closed deals are dimmed but
still selectable: choose an open stage to correct a mistaken close. Any member can update a live
deal, whoever owns it.

## Log what changed

Turn on the tiles you need: **Note**, **Value**, **Stage**, **Follow-up**. Fill them in and press
**Save** once. A note records what happened with the customer. Values use the deal's existing
currency; Stage lists the open stages. A follow-up creates a todo in your Next actions, owned by
you and linked to this deal and its contact.

**Mark Won** asks for the closing day, today or earlier. **Mark Lost** asks for an optional reason;
a reason is stored on the deal and added to its notes. Both buttons also save the note, value and
follow-up you filled in, with the close determining the stage. See [Stages and closing](04-stages-and-closing.md).

## Tell Baker, then check the preview

If your install has an AI provider, the **Or just tell Baker** box accepts a sentence such as
“sent samples, move to Proposal, value 2500”. Press **Ask** to see the exact changes and the deal
they belong to. Read each line, then **Confirm** to save or **Cancel** to discard the preview.
Asking alone changes nothing. Confirm uses the same save path as the tiles.

Baker copies the note from your sentence. If you only gave instructions and did not say what
happened, it asks for one line before you can confirm. A Won preview displays today's date when
you ask and keeps that explicit date when you confirm. Choose Mark Won instead to backdate a win.
An already-closed deal is not closed again by a preview, and an existing lost reason is kept.

Your confirmation makes this your edit, so it carries no “Baker wrote this” badge. With no AI
provider, the box is hidden; the rest of Quick update still works. Read [Meet Baker](../07-baker/01-meet-baker.md)
for the separate chat experience.

## When a save fails

A refused save writes nothing. All deal fields, notes and the follow-up save in one transaction,
and your entries stay on screen after a refusal. If the connection fails or the server cannot
confirm the outcome, open the deal and check what is there before trying again. The screen never
automatically retries a save.
