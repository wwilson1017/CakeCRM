Baker carries a small library: CakeCRM's own help topics, and a shelf of sales playbooks. There is no separate screen for either. You reach them by asking Baker.

## Asking how CakeCRM works

Ask Baker how something in the app works, such as "How do notifications work?" or "Who can connect Gmail?", and it answers from CakeCRM's built-in help rather than guessing. When a task is admin-only, Baker should say so up front.

## The playbooks

Each playbook is an own-words summary of a published book: its core ideas, when Baker reaches for it, how it applies inside the CRM, and a pointer to go and read the original.

| Book | Author | Reach for it when |
|---|---|---|
| The Challenger Sale | Matthew Dixon and Brent Adamson | The buyer likes you but nothing moves. |
| Fanatical Prospecting | Jeb Blount | Your pipeline is thin and needs a steady prospecting habit. |
| Gap Selling | Keenan | You need to find the real problem and the value of solving it. |
| Getting to Yes | Roger Fisher and William Ury with Bruce Patton | You are negotiating and want to work from interests, not positions. |
| How to Win Friends and Influence People | Dale Carnegie | A first meeting, or a relationship that needs repair. |
| Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion | Robert B. Cialdini | You want to frame a follow-up honestly and persuasively. |
| The JOLT Effect | Matthew Dixon and Ted McKenna | A late-stage deal is stuck on the buyer's indecision. |
| Never Split the Difference | Chris Voss with Tahl Raz | Pushback, a price objection, or a contact who has gone quiet. |
| SPIN Selling | Neil Rackham | A discovery call, or an early deal with no stated problem. |
| To Sell Is Human | Daniel H. Pink | Reading the buyer, handling rejection and finding the real problem. |

## Using them

You do not have to name a book. Describe the situation, for example "David Kim keeps pushing back on price" or "this deal has gone quiet", and Baker looks up the technique that fits. When it uses a playbook, Baker names the book and its author, so you know where the advice comes from and can read further.

The playbooks need an AI provider, because Baker is the only way to reach them. See [AI setup](06-ai-setup.md).
